Privacy Policy for Block Legend

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Block Legend (the “App”), developed and published by TwishGames (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use it on Android devices.

In short:
  • No account, sign-up, name, email or phone number is needed to play.
  • We do not run any servers and we do not collect, receive or sell your personal data.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. You can control this in the App’s Ad Privacy setting.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information we collect

We, TwishGames, do not collect personal information. The App has no login, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and does not send your game data to us.

Some information is collected by the third-party services built into the App, described in Section 3. Those services act under their own privacy policies.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device only, so you can continue where you left off:

  • Game progress: unlocked Adventure stages, stars earned and best scores.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration and language choices, and which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  • Whether you have purchased “Remove Ads”.

This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID.
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location (country or city).
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped.
  • Diagnostic information related to ad performance.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · How Google uses data for advertising.

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time (see Section 5).

Google Play Billing (purchases)

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never receive or store your card or payment information. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Unity (game engine)

The App is built with the Unity engine, which may send anonymous technical information, such as device model, operating system version and graphics hardware, to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Ad privacy: open Settings → Ad Privacy in the App to review or change your ad consent choices.
  • Advertising ID: in Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Remove ads: buying “Remove Ads” stops ads from being shown in the App.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the App, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete all game data stored on your device.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any of your data, because we do not receive it. Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Data collected by Google or Unity is kept according to their privacy policies; you can manage your Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google through the links above. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
