# Privacy Policy for Block Legend

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application **Block Legend** ("Application") created by **TwishGames** ("we", "us", or "our").

---

### 1. What Information Does the Application Collect?

#### A. Information You Provide
The Application does not require any personal registration or personal identification (such as name, email address, or phone number) to play.

#### B. Automatically Collected Information
The Application may collect certain non-personally identifiable technical information automatically, including:
* The type of mobile device you use (e.g. Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel).
* Your mobile operating system version (e.g. Android 14).
* Generic performance and crash diagnostics.

---

### 2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The Application displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. These third-party ad networks may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID) in accordance with their respective privacy policies to deliver contextually relevant advertisements and fraud prevention.

For more information regarding Google's data handling practices, please review:
* [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Google Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

---

### 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application is designed for general audiences and casual puzzle game players. We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us, and we will take immediate steps to delete such information.

---

### 4. In-App Purchases & Billing

All transactions for in-game purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed securely through the **Google Play In-App Billing** service. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card details or financial information.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Security

Game progression, high scores, and local preferences are stored locally on your device via encrypted local storage and are not transmitted to any external private server.
